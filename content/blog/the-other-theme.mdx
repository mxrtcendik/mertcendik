title: Check the Other Theme

---

These two bars use different grays. Switch the theme and look again.

<ContrastDemo />

One of them is the theme's muted color. On a dark page it sits clearly above the background. On a white page it is close enough to the background that a track, a code chip, or a skeleton can look unfinished. The other is mixed from the foreground, about 8%. It stays visible in both themes because it is defined as a step away from the page, not as a fixed gray that happened to look right in the theme you built first.

I design in one theme and check the other before I call it done. Not a screenshot at the end of the week. The same screen, toggled, while the work is still open. The things that fail are usually the quiet ones: a border that is 8% white, a hover that is a whisper, a placeholder that was tuned on black.

Text has a harder bar than a decorative bar. Body copy should meet contrast in both themes. A track only has to be seen. I still want to see it without hunting.

If a color only works in one theme, it is not a color of the product yet. It is a color of the mock.
